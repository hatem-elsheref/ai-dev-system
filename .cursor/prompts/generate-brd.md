# Generate BRD

Create a Business Requirements Document for the current project.

## Include
- Business context
- Problem statement
- Objectives and KPIs
- Target users/personas
- Core business workflows
- Constraints and assumptions
- Success criteria

## Output Format
- Title
- Business Background
- Problem and Opportunity
- Goals and KPIs
- Stakeholders and Personas
- Scope (In/Out)
- High-Level Requirements
- Acceptance Criteria
